# Risk Hub basics

## Where things are in the Risk Hub
Open the left menu, choose Security and Compliance, then Risk Hub. The Risk Hub has these pages, and you only see the ones your role allows:
- Dashboard: risk posture at a glance.
- Risk Registers: every risk you can see, across the approval workflow.
- Add Risk: raise a new risk.
- Analytics: trends, the workflow funnel and compliance coverage, with CSV export.
- Audit Trail: every change made to the risks you can see, with who changed what and when.
The Admin Console is a separate item in the left menu, for managing users and reference data. The User Guide link is at the bottom of the left menu. The round Ask AI button at the bottom right of every Risk Hub page opens the Risk assistant, which answers questions and shows numbers, lists and charts for the risks you have access to.

## How to add a risk
1. In the left menu, open Risk Hub, then Add Risk. If you do not see Add Risk, your role does not allow raising risks.
2. Step 1, Basic Information: fill in Year, Quarter, Source Register, Risk Title, Risk Description, Compliance References (optional), Risk Category, Accepted By, Identified By, Risk Identified Date and Risk Assigned To. Click Next.
3. Step 2, Risk Assessment: click the cell in the risk matrix that matches the Likelihood and Impact, write the Impact Description, and set the Implementation Date and the Reassessment Date. Click Next.
4. Step 3, Risk Treatment Plan: choose the Assignment Team, Risk Owner and Action Owner, pick the Treatment Strategy (Remediate, Accept, Transfer or Avoid), write the action plan description and its steps, and fill in the Email Subject. A Management Approver is required when the risk is High.
5. Click Submit. The risk then goes to the Risk Owner for approval.
Required fields are marked with an asterisk, and the form tells you what is missing when you click Next or Submit.

## How to find a risk and open its details
Open Risk Hub, then Risk Registers. Use the tabs to narrow by stage: Approved Risks, Pending Risk Owner Approval, Pending Management Approval, Pending Compliance Approval, Pending Revision and Overdue Risks. Use the search box to find a risk by its code or title, and the Register, Residual Level, Treatment Strategy, Risk Type and Status filters to narrow further. Click the eye icon at the end of a row to open the risk's details: its status, scores, people, action plans and history.

## What you can do on a risk
Open the risk from Risk Registers (the eye icon). The buttons at the bottom of the details panel depend on your role and on the risk's status. They can include Edit Risk, Cancel Risk, Assess Risk (to reassess it), Escalate, Add Action Plan and Submit for Approval, and approvers also see buttons to approve or reject. If a button is missing, your role or the risk's current status does not allow that action.

## How a risk is approved
A new risk goes through these stages in order: Pending Risk Owner Approval, then Pending Management Approval (for High risks), then Pending Compliance Review, then Approved, which is shown as Open while its action plan is carried out. When the work is done it goes through Pending Owner Completion Approval, Pending Management Closure Approval (for High risks) and Pending Compliance Closure, and then it is Closed. A risk can also be sent back for changes (Pending Revision or Pending Amendment), Escalated when it is overdue, or Cancelled.

## Who does what
- The Risk Assigner raises the risk, picks the owner and action owner, writes the action plan and submits it.
- The Risk Owner approves or rejects the risks they own and records reassessments.
- Management approves or rejects High risks and handles escalations.
- The Compliance team reviews and closes risks.
- The Action Owner carries out the action plan steps and uploads evidence.

## Dashboard and analytics
The Dashboard shows the number of Total, Open, Closed and Overdue risks, charts of status and treatment strategy, and a heat map of Likelihood by Impact. The Register filter at the top right narrows it to one register. Analytics shows trends over time, the workflow funnel and compliance coverage, and can be exported as CSV. To download a report of risks, use the Report Download button at the top right of Risk Registers.

## AI help in the Risk Hub
The Ask AI button opens the Risk assistant. Ask it for numbers, breakdowns, charts or lists of risks, or ask it how to do something. It only ever covers the risks you have access to. On Add Risk, AI suggestion buttons can propose a category, a likelihood and an action plan description when they are switched on; they are advice only and you decide what to keep.
